Course Contents

auto_fix_high Edited by AI: Structured 5-unit modular pacing (42 lecture hrs) with integrated lab cross-references
Unit I

Foundations of Distributed Systems & Clock Models

8 Lecture Hrs

Architectural models (client-server, peer-to-peer), Network communication abstractions, Physical clocks vs Lamport logical clocks, Vector timestamps, Global snapshot algorithms (Chandy-Lamport).

Unit II

Consensus, Leader Election & Replication Protocols

9 Lecture Hrs

Mutual exclusion, Bully and Ring election algorithms, CAP theorem & PACELC formalizations, Multi-Paxos vs Raft state machine replication, Linearizability vs Sequential consistency.

Unit III

Fault Tolerance, Transactions & Byzantine Agreement

8 Lecture Hrs

Failure detectors, Two-Phase Commit (2PC) and Three-Phase Commit (3PC), Byzantine Fault Tolerance (PBFT), Quorum-based voting systems, Self-stabilizing topologies.

Unit IV

Cloud Infrastructure, Containers & Microservice Autoscaling

9 Lecture Hrs +14 Lab Hrs

IaaS/PaaS/Serverless paradigms, Docker container mechanics, Kubernetes orchestration pod primitives, Service meshes (Envoy/Istio), Rate limiting, Circuit-breaking, and horizontal autoscaling (HPA).

science Lab Module: Distributed gRPC service mesh with dynamic failure injection and automated pod failover.
Unit V

Distributed Storage, Cloud Security & SLA Compliance

8 Lecture Hrs +14 Lab Hrs

Distributed file systems (GFS, HDFS, Ceph), NoSQL partition patterns, Dynamo ring hash partitioning, Mutual TLS (mTLS), Identity federation, Distributed tracing (OpenTelemetry), 99.999% SLA modeling.

science Lab Module: Multi-region consistent hash ring with anti-entropy gossip replication in Go.
